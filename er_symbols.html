ER Diagram Symbols (Chen notation)

Every symbol from your lectures, drawn. Blue 1 / N / M are cardinality labels.

Entity type

Rectangle. A kind of thing we store data about, e.g. EMPLOYEE.

Weak entity type

Double rectangle. Has no key of its own and depends on an owner entity.

Attribute

Oval. A property of an entity, e.g. Salary.

Key attribute

Oval with the name underlined. Unique for every entity, e.g. Ssn.

Composite attribute

Oval with more ovals attached for its parts, e.g. Name = Fname + Lname.

Multi-valued attribute

Double oval. Can hold many values, e.g. Phone.

Derived attribute

Dashed oval. Calculated from another attribute, e.g. Age from Date of birth.

Partial key

Oval with a dashed underline. Identifies a weak entity only together with the owner's key.

Relationship type

Diamond. Relates two or more entity types.

Identifying relationship

Double diamond. Connects a weak entity to its owner.

Partial participation

Single line. Optional: an entity may take no part (zero or more). Not existence-dependent.

Total participation

Double line. Mandatory: every entity must take part (one or more). Existence-dependent.

Cardinality ratio

1, N or M written on the line = the maximum number on each side (here N:1).

Attribute on a relationship

Oval joined to the diamond, for data that belongs to the pair, e.g. Hours.

Binary relationshipMCQ

Degree 2: connects two entity types.

Ternary relationshipMCQ

Degree 3: one diamond joins three entity types (n-ary = n entity types).
